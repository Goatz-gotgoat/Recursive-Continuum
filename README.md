Recursive Continuum Field

An interactive 3D conceptual visualization of distinct recursive structures occupying a shared spatial field.

Structure

24 figure-eight systems: Four rotating paths in each of six clusters.

Two axes: Four clusters across the horizontal axis and two across the vertical.

Central nexus: The moving volumes overlap around a common center, surrounded by a golden particle field.

Motion: Counter-rotating figure-eights retain recognizable local forms while their relative orientations change.

Run

Open Recursive Continuum Field.html in a modern WebGL-capable browser. The original loads Three.js from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed. Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom. Desktop performance may be smoother than mobile because of the high particle count.

Golden-ratio rotations

The author has updated the original to use exact golden-ratio expressions, with (\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2) and the four signed rates proportional to

[
1,\quad -\varphi^{-1},\quad \varphi^{-2},\quad -\varphi^{-3}.
]

This four-frequency pattern repeats across clusters. It creates differing individual rhythms and exact higher-order phase relationships. It does not mathematically prevent every temporary alignment or resonance.

Mathematical scope

The original figure is a kinematic visualization: its rotations are prescribed, and overlapping particles do not by themselves demonstrate physical interaction.

Recursive_Nexus_Coupled_3D.html is a separate experimental prototype with optional reciprocal phase coupling, perturbation controls, and diagnostic measurements. Its results are illustrative and have not established a physical nexus or a unique advantage for golden-ratio frequencies.

The original geometry and visual design can be retained without introducing the experimental coupling model.
